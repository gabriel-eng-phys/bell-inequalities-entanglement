# Bell Inequalities and Quantum Entanglement

Undergraduate quantum physics project carried out at the
Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC).

The project reviews the historical and theoretical development of the
EPR paradox, Bell's theorem and quantum entanglement, and includes
numerical simulations of Bell-type inequalities.

## Project contents

- Theoretical discussion of the EPR paradox and Bell's theorem;
- Derivations and discussion of Bell and CHSH inequalities;
- Numerical analysis of Bell–Wigner and CHSH inequalities;
- Simulation of entangled two-qubit systems using Python and Qibo;
- Comparison between classical bounds and quantum-mechanical predictions;
- Discussion of noise, statistical uncertainty and experimental Bell tests.
